How Brieffolio fits in

We handle the lift.
You make the call.

The goal is simple: turn a recurring time-sink into a quick review step — without losing your firm's point of view.

Step 01

We learn your voice

Share a few past commentaries, brand guidelines, and any guardrails from your CIO or compliance team.

  • Map your tone and house views to our engine
  • Agree on structure, length, and data sources
  • Nothing goes to clients without your sign-off
Step 02

Monthly briefs on a schedule

Once a month we pull fresh data, generate your brief pack, and send drafts to your team for quick review.

  • Delivery timed to CPI, jobs, and Fed meeting dates
  • Most teams tweak 5–10% before sending
  • Advisors get a predictable, reusable toolkit every month
