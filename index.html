How Brieffolio fits in

We handle the lift.
You keep the point of view.

The goal is simple: turn a recurring communication lift into a fast review step without flattening your firm's voice or perspective.

Step 01

We learn your voice

Share a few past updates, brand guidelines, and any internal guardrails from leadership, compliance, or investor relations.

  • Map your tone and house views to our workflow
  • Agree on structure, length, and data sources
  • Nothing goes out without your sign-off
Step 02

Monthly briefs on a schedule

Each month we pull fresh data, generate your communication pack, and send drafts to your team for quick review and final approval.

  • Timing can align to CPI, jobs, Fed dates, or your own reporting cadence
  • Most teams only tweak a small portion before sending
  • Your team gets a predictable, reusable communication toolkit
