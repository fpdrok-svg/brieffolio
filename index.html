How Brieffolio fits into your month

We handle the heavy lift. You make the call.

The goal is simple: turn a recurring time sink into a quick review step without losing your point of view.

1. We learn your voice

You share a handful of past commentaries, brand guidelines, and any red‑lines from your CIO or compliance team.

  • We map your tone, house views, and language to our engine
  • We agree on length, structure, and data sources up front
  • Nothing goes to clients without your sign‑off

2. Monthly briefs on a schedule

Once a month we pull fresh data, generate your email + PDF + notes, and send drafts to your team for quick review.

  • Set delivery windows that line up with CPI/jobs/Fed meetings
  • Edits and nuance are easy—most teams tweak 5–10%
  • Your advisors get a predictable, reusable toolkit every month
